# Trigger-Bot-2
This is a new & improved Discord bot based of off the anime 'World Trigger'.

Here are all the slash commands in Trigger Bot:

**Getting Started**
- `/joinborder` — Register as a Border agent

**Profile & Stats**
- `/profile` — View your profile card
- `/stats` — View your agent stats
- `/upgradestat` — Spend a stat point on a stat

**Triggers**
- `/shop` — Browse available triggers
- `/buytrigger` — Buy a trigger with credits
- `/loadout` — View your equipped triggers
- `/equip` — Equip a trigger into a slot (Main / Sub / Optional)

**Combat**
- `/arena` — Enter Solo Arena matchmaking (PvP or PvE fallback)

**Progression**
- `/spin` — Reroll your Trion or Side Effect
- `/story` — View your current story mission
- `/mission` — Start your current story mission

**Squads**
- `/squadcreate` — Create a squad
- `/squadinvite` — Invite a player to your squad
- `/squadinfo` — View your squad details
- `/squadleave` — Leave your squad

**Leaderboard**
- `/leaderboard` — Top 10 agents by ELO
