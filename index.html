COMMAND LIST

/joinborderRegister as a Border agent. Rolls your Trion level and Side Effect. Grants 5 spins and 100 credits.
/setclass [class]Choose your combat class: Attacker, Sniper, Gunner, Shooter, or All Rounder.
/faction [name]Join Kido, Shinoda, or Tamakoma. Each grants a passive stat buff.
/profileView your full agent profile including stats, loadout, story progress, and daily missions.
/aboutView bot info and feature overview.
/helpShow a compact command reference list.
/arenaEnter the turn-based solo arena against Neighbor waves. 30s cooldown. Win to earn ELO, stat points, trigger XP.
/duel [@user]Challenge another agent to a turn-based PvP duel. They must accept within 60 seconds.
/missionAccept a random defense mission — Civilian Rescue, Neighbor Ambush, Supply Defence, or HQ Breach Response.
/storyView your current story mission details and rewards.
/storymissionPlay your current story chapter. Boss battles, exploration, and branching choices.
/expeditionB-Rank+ only. Launch a 4-hour expedition for big credit and spin rewards.
/bailoutEmergency bail-out. Costs 1/3 of your current Trion.
/simulationPreview your equipped loadout in a risk-free simulation.
/combostatsPreview your estimated damage output with your current build.
/shopBrowse the paginated Border Trigger Shop. Use ◀/▶ to page through all triggers.
/buytrigger [name]Purchase a trigger from the shop using credits.
/equip [trigger] [slot]Equip a trigger into Main, Sub, or Optional slot. Main-type triggers go in Main/Sub only.
/loadoutView your current trigger loadout across all three slots.
/triggerforge [t1] [t2]Fuse two compatible main triggers into a powerful combined weapon.
/dismantle [name]Break a combined trigger back into its two components.
/sideeffectsBrowse the side effects index with rarity info.
/spin [type]Spend a spin to reroll your Trion level or Side Effect.
/triggers_masteredView your trigger mastery levels and XP progress.
/statsView your attack, defense, mobility, intelligence, trion control, and perception stats.
/upgradestat [stat]Spend a stat point to increase a stat. Cap scales with rank (C: 15, B: 30, A: 50).
/skilltreeView your class's skill tree and current progress.
/upgradeskill [name]Spend skill points to level up a class skill.
/trionrankView your current Border rank (C/B/A) and what's needed to advance.
/leaderboardView the top 10 agents by ELO ranking.
/missionsboardView today's daily missions and reward progress.
/squadcreate [name]Create a new squad. You become the leader.
/squadinvite [@user]Leaders only. Invite a Border agent to your squad (max 5 members).
/squadinfoView your squad's name, division, ELO, operator, and all members.
/squadleaveLeave your current squad.
/operator [name]Assign Shiori, Asami, or Hana as your squad's operator. Leaders only.
/trainersView available Border trainers and their costs.
/train [trainer]Pay credits to train with Shinoda, Kido, Karasuma, or Yūma for direct stat boosts.
/redeem [code]Redeem a special code for credits, spins, or triggers.
/neighborhoodScout your area for Neighbor threat level, HP, and damage stats.
/baseView Border Base HP and defense level.
/baseinfoView total agents, squads, and credits in circulation server-wide.
/classesFull class matchup chart with advantage descriptions.
/pingCheck bot latency in milliseconds.

HOW TO PLAY

Step 01
Join Border

Register as an agent. You'll receive a random Trion level (2–38), a possible Side Effect, 5 spins, and 100 credits.

/joinborder
Step 02
Choose Class & Faction

Pick from Attacker, Sniper, Gunner, Shooter, or All Rounder. Each has a rock-paper-scissors advantage. Then join a faction for passive buffs.

/setclass · /faction
Step 03
Buy & Equip Triggers

Browse the shop and buy triggers with credits. Equip a Main trigger and an Optional. Main triggers have combat moves; Optionals give passive buffs.

/shop · /buytrigger · /equip
Step 04
Fight & Earn

Enter the arena or take missions to fight Neighbors turn-by-turn. Win to earn ELO, credits, stat points, skill points, and trigger mastery XP.

/arena · /mission
Step 05
Level Up Your Build

Spend stat points on attack, mobility, and trion control. Unlock skills in your class tree. Master triggers to unlock higher-damage moves.

/upgradestat · /upgradeskill
Step 06
Form a Squad

Create a squad, invite allies, and assign an operator. Operators provide in-battle abilities: damage boosts, strikes, and cooldown reduction.

/squadcreate · /operator
Step 07
Reach A-Rank

Climb from C-Rank (ELO 0–1199) to B-Rank (1200+) to A-Rank (1600+). Higher ranks unlock bigger stat caps and expedition access.

/trionrank · /leaderboard
Step 08
Play Story Mode

Work through the Prologue arc — exploration, choices, boss battles against Aftokrator Black Trigger users. Complete them to earn rare triggers.

/story · /storymission
